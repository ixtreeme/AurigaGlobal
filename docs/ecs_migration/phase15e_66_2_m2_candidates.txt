# Phase 15E-66.2 - M2 Signature Candidates

Date: 2026-05-02
Scope: M2 service-call bridge signature review and safe migration.

## Baseline

- Baseline after 15E-66.1: 127 broad `LPCHARACTER` / `CHARACTER*` matches in ECS files.
- Current pre-66.2 measured broad count: 128. The delta comes from the broad regex including local variables and helper definitions, not only public signatures.

## Deferred Candidates

These were reviewed and intentionally not migrated in 15E-66.2:

- `PlayerRuntimeSystem.cpp` legacy method bodies such as `CHARACTER::GetQuestNPC`, `CHARACTER::GetSoulItemDamage`, `CHARACTER::SetShopOwner`, marry partner methods, event callbacks, dynamic pointer assignment and `GET_SEX`.
- `InventorySystem.cpp` `CItem::AddToCharacter`, `DistanceValid`, `IsOwnership`, `SetOwnership`, `CanUsedBy`, `FindEquipCell`, `EquipTo`, and unequip local owner handling.
- `NetworkSyncSystem.cpp`, `MovementSystem.cpp`, `ActivitySystem.cpp`, `CombatSystem.cpp`: higher M3 ratio, deferred to subsystem-specific phases.

## Result

- Broad count after 66.2 migrated candidates: 117.
- Reduction: 11 broad matches.
- The result is lower than the initial target because the remaining matches are mostly M3 legacy method bodies and event/callback bridge code, not safe M2 wrapper signatures.

## Recommendation

- Do not chase the numeric under-80 target by hiding `LPCHARACTER` behind `auto*` in M3 code.
- Proceed with a formal M3 defer list, then schedule subsystem refactor phases:
  - NetworkSync service abstraction.
  - Movement callback/service abstraction.
  - Inventory/CItem service abstraction.
  - Combat subsystem extraction.

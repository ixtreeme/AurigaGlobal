Phase 29 - Costume mount no longer leaves a duplicate follower
Date: 2026-09-22
Report: one mount appeared twice - the player rode one model while an identical
mount creature kept following.

Root cause
----------
The costume mount migration (76b571fc) moved the ridden/follower split to
ecs::CostumeMountRuntime but dropped two pieces of the legacy CMountSystem::

    CMountSystem::Mount(vnum, item)
      -> UnsummonByItem(item); UnsummonAll();   // follower entities destroyed
      -> CMountActor::Mount(item)
           -> ClearHorse(owner);                // regular horse dismissed

The new MountCostume only applied AFFECT_MOUNT (which sets the rider's mount
model through POINT_MOUNT) and kept the follower creature alive, so the rider
was rendered on the mount while the follower creature walked behind. The same
gap existed in CheckMount (called from affect changes): mid-ride it could
summon the follower back, and the follow update kept a stale follower for a
rider who had mounted by another path.

Fix (ecs/systems/MountSystem.cpp)
---------------------------------
- HideCostumeRecord/HideCostumeFollowers destroy the follower creature while
  keeping the record and its item association, so UnmountCostume re-summons the
  same mount. This is the native form of the legacy actor Unsummon.
- MountCostume now clears the regular horse first (StopRiding + dismiss the
  summoned horse) and hides every follower after the mount affects are applied.
- UpdateCostumeMounts hides followers while the rider is mounted and does not
  retire the hidden records, so a stale follower cannot survive the ride.
- CheckMount returns while mounted, so an affect change mid-ride cannot spawn a
  second visible mount.
- UnmountCostume already re-summons from the kept record; no change needed.

Behaviour notes
---------------
- The follower is hidden for any active mount (costume or horse), matching the
  "one visible mount while riding" rule; it returns on dismount.
- Equipping/using a costume mount still auto-mounts, as the legacy equip path
  did; it now dismisses the horse and hides the follower in the same step.

Verification
------------
- GameServer Release builds; the whole suite (29 targets) passes.
- build-asan GameServer builds.
- No headless costume-mount fixture exists (MountLifecycleTests documents that
  the costume mount runtime is GameServer-covered); the in-game check is:
  wear a costume mount item, summon a regular horse, mount and dismount, and
  confirm exactly one mount is visible while riding and the follower returns
  after the dismount.

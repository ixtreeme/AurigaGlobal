[X_PADDING=12;COLOR=ffff00]START:
[X_PADDING=12]Level up on Map1 until at least level 25–30. During this, smash Metin Stones to get Blessing Scrolls and upgrade materials.
[X_PADDING=20]Don’t skip Brutal Captains – they can drop level 30 weapons.
[X_PADDING=20]At level 26, it’s worth buying a level 26 armor and level 21 helmet from the NPC, which you can easily upgrade to +9.

[X_PADDING=12;COLOR=ffff00]LVL 30:
[X_PADDING=12]Go to Map2 for further leveling and farming. If you have solid gear (armor, helmet, level 30 weapon), level 45 Metins won’t be an issue.
[X_PADDING=20]While farming, don’t forget the dungeons! Use the time limit and complete them
[X_PADDING=20]to earn valuable mounts, pets, and more.
[X_PADDING=20]From Orc Metins, you’ll need a level 42 armor and level 41 shield to survive on the next map!

[X_PADDING=12;COLOR=ffff00]LVL 50:
[X_PADDING=20]By now, you should have at least one pet and six mounts in your mount inventory — each dungeon gives you two.
[X_PADDING=20]Level up to 65 and don’t forget about the Open Boss – you can obtain valuable legendary bonus adders from it!
[X_PADDING=20]You’ll need a level 65 weapon; once upgraded to +9, it gives +55% Devil Damage.
[X_PADDING=20]You can also obtain level 65 armor from the Open Boss – essential for accessing the next map.
